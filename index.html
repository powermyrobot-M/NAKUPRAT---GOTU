Rooted in Culture, United by Peace

Borana, Turkana and Samburu traditions thrive together. From colorful shukas to beaded manyattas, our heritage shapes conservation across 39,300 hectares of community trust land.

Women Empowerment

Beadwork cooperatives and savings groups transforming livelihoods across Ngare Mara, Akunoit, and Gotu settlements.

Manyatta Life

Pastoralist traditions meet modern conservation — semi-nomadic communities practicing sustainable grazing for decades.

Peace Sports Events

Annual inter-community games uniting Borana, Turkana & Samburu — ending cattle rustling through friendship.

Guardians of the Rangelands

29 community rangers patrol daily, protecting wildlife corridors and building peace across 12 zones.

Wildlife We Protect

From reticulated giraffes to Grevy's zebra — our conservancy secures critical habitat and migration corridors.

Reticulated Giraffe

Iconic herds moving through Boji Dera, Jajaab and Shaba National Reserve corridors.

Grevy's Zebra

Endangered species protected in Chafa Gafarsa — one of Kenya's last strongholds.

Beisa Oryx & Lions
